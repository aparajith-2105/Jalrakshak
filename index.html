Model and data notes

This standalone dashboard trains a browser-based k-nearest-neighbors classifier from data/irrigation_prediction.csv. It needs no server-side prediction API.

  • Target labels are Low, Medium, and High irrigation need; they are not verified liters or valve runtimes.
  • The browser loads the included labeled examples and compares a field's inputs with similar examples.
  • Optional sensor checks use freshness, range, peer agreement, repeated samples, and relative offset rules. They flag possible sensor problems, not confirmed failures.
  • Plant moisture logs contain no positive irrigation labels in the supplied rows; they are not treated as training labels.
  • IOT-temp.csv contains air temperature rather than soil moisture. The 2025 weather snapshot is not current weather.
  • NASA POWER daily weather is optional and requires internet access and correct coordinates.

The training CSV and prediction run in your browser. History is kept in this browser only.
