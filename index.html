Model and data notes

This dashboard trains a Random Forest classifier from irrigation_prediction.csv.

  • Target labels are Low, Medium, and High irrigation need; they are not verified liters or valve runtimes.
  • Class counts are imbalanced, so the model page should be reviewed alongside its evaluation metrics.
  • Plant moisture logs contain no positive irrigation labels in the supplied rows; they are not treated as training labels.
  • IOT-temp.csv contains air temperature rather than soil moisture. The 2025 weather snapshot is not current weather.
  • NASA POWER daily weather is optional and requires internet access and correct coordinates.

Model evaluation metrics are returned by the first prediction request.
